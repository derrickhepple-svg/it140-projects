## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

Blood Moon Manor is a vampire-themed horror adventure game set inside an old and mysterious manor.

**Storyline:**

The player is a newly awakened vampire trapped inside Blood Moon Manor while a vampire hunter searches the estate. To survive, the player must explore the manor and collect six items: an ancient tome, blood vial, silver dagger, protective amulet, cloak, and crypt key. After collecting all six items, the player will be prepared to confront the vampire hunter. If the player encounters the hunter before collecting all of the items, the player loses the game.

## Rooms

Project One requires a minimum of eight rooms.

1. Grand Foyer - Start room
2. Library
3. Dining Hall
4. Armory
5. Crypt
6. Servants' Quarters
7. Study
8. Courtyard - Villain room

## Items

With the minimum eight-room design, Project One requires at least six items.
Every room except the start room and villain room must contain one item.

1. Ancient Tome - Library
2. Blood Vial - Dining Hall
3. Silver Dagger - Armory
4. Protective Amulet - Crypt
5. Cloak - Servants' Quarters
6. Crypt Key - Study

## Villain

The villain is a vampire hunter who has entered Blood Moon Manor to destroy the player. The player must avoid the vampire hunter until all six items have been collected. Encountering the vampire hunter before collecting all six items causes the player to lose the game.
